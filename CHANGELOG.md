# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Save links, text, pictures, videos, files and notes, from the app or from any app's share sheet.
- Link previews and readable copies fetched when online, with embedded players for YouTube, Instagram and other platforms.
- Search across titles, notes, page text and the text inside pictures and PDFs, read on the phone.
- Spaces and Smart Spaces, tags, pinning, multi-select, Recently deleted for 30 days.
- Sort by newest, oldest, title or site; cards or list view; Undo after a delete.
- Back up and restore in one file. Import browser bookmarks or a mymind export.
- Light, dark and OLED black, six colour schemes, text size, corner roundness, grid columns, motion and haptic settings, six app icons.
- Material 3 Expressive toolbar, slide transitions and predictive back.
- Works on Android 8.0 and newer.
